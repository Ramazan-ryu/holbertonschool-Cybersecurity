## Post-Incident Analysis: The Handshake Deal

**1. Factual Summary**
NorthBay Consulting commenced a penetration test based solely on verbal authorization from the client’s CTO, who lacked the legal authority to approve the engagement. During the test, consultants extracted customer data, triggering internal alarms that led the uninformed General Counsel to report a data breach to regulatory authorities. Upon discovering the activity was a third-party test, the client issued a cease-and-desist, and NorthBay faced severe legal and regulatory fallout.

**2. Rule, Law, and Standard Violations**
*   **Legal:** Violation of the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) / Computer Misuse Act (or regional equivalents) regarding unauthorized access. Verbal permission from an unauthorized party does not constitute legal authorization.
*   **Professional Standards:** Violation of PTES Pre-engagement Interactions and CREST Code of Conduct, which strictly mandate formal, written authorization and clearly defined Rules of Engagement (RoE) prior to any active testing.

**3. Correct Professional Behavior**
NorthBay Consulting should have halted the engagement process until a formal, written authorization letter and Statement of Work (SOW) were signed by an executive with the legal authority to bind the company and assume risk (e.g., the CEO, Board, or General Counsel). Furthermore, the consulting firm should have required that key stakeholders, including the incident response team and legal department, be aware of the testing window to prevent false breach reports.

**4. Preventative Pre-Engagement Clauses**
*   **Executive Authorization Requirement:** A clause stating, "Testing will not commence under any circumstances until this document is physically or digitally signed by a C-level executive or designated legal representative possessing the authority to authorize third-party access to the organization's network."
*   **Internal Notification Acknowledgment:** A clause requiring the client to confirm that internal security, legal, and operational teams have been notified of the testing windows and source IP addresses.
