# Engagement Brief - Halcyon Cloud

Client: Halcyon Cloud, a payroll/HR SaaS in Bangalore. A previous low-cost vendor
delivered a two-hundred-finding scan with no proof. Ridgeline has been brought in
to verify. You receive the prior findings and a live slice of the estate.

Your lead is Karim Haddad. For every finding he asks two questions: show me the
proof, and tell me exactly where you stopped. A scanner finding is a hypothesis;
verification is the experiment that proves it true or false. If you did not run
the experiment, you do not have a finding.
