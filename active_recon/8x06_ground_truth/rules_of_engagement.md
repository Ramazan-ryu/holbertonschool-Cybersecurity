# Rules of Engagement (signed)

Authorised: discovery, enumeration, web mapping, bespoke-protocol assessment,
analysis of the provided reports, hand verification of findings, and business-risk
prioritisation against the live Castellan estate, within scope.

Forbidden: exploitation for impact of any kind - database dumping, broad record
enumeration, command execution, grid-control commands, credential theft, denial of
service, weaponised payloads, real sensitive-data access, exploit frameworks. Each
phase states its stop-point. Confirm presence and prioritise risk; impact is a
later engagement's work.
