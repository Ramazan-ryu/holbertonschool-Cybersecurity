# Engagement Brief - Castellan Energy

Client: Castellan Energy, a regional power utility (critical infrastructure) in
Vienna. The estate is mixed and real: corporate/customer web, internal network
services, network infrastructure and a bespoke operational service that runs part
of the grid. Some systems carry the business (grid operations, the billing
platform with account/payment data); others are peripheral (a marketing site,
ageing hosts). A documented asset-criticality model says which is which and is
the basis for prioritisation.

You have an authorised scope, a live estate, three pre-run scanner reports and the
asset/threat-context data. Assess and prioritise; do not exploit. Deliver one
report two audiences can act on. Your lead, Karim Haddad, signs it.
