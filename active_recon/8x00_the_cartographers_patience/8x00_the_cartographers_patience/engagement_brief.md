# Engagement Brief — Berent Industrial

Client: Berent Industrial, industrial automation manufacturer, Kraków.
Consultancy: Ridgeline Security. Lead: Karim Haddad.
Your role: junior penetration tester, scanning phase.

The Berent estate sits on a segmented network behind a defended perimeter,
mixing modern infrastructure with fragile legacy controllers. Some hosts block
ICMP. Some services run on non-standard ports. The perimeter's filtering policy
is for you to deduce, and it carries at least one naive filter rule. An IDS
watches scan rate.

Authorised scope: the 10.10.10.0/24 subnet only. The full host inventory is NOT
published; discovering it is the work. Deliver a host inventory, a port-state
map, a service inventory, a firewall analysis and a scan report the next team
can trust without re-verifying.
