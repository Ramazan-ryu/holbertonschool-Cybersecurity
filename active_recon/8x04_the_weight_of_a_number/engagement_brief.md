# Engagement Brief - Aurum Pay

Client: Aurum Pay, a Sao Paulo payment processor. The assessed estate is a
representative slice: a customer-facing web app, internal services, a payment
database (the cardholder data environment / crown jewel), network infrastructure,
a marketing site and a decommissioned host.

A scanning pass is already done. You receive the three scanners' reports, the
asset-criticality model and threat-context data (KEV, EPSS). The Rules of
Engagement authorise active verification of contested findings against a
lightweight copy of the estate. You do NOT run the scanners.

Your lead is Karim Haddad. He reads the order you assign and the justification
behind it, not the scanner's number. A CVSS base score is severity, identical in
every environment by design. Risk is what a vulnerability weighs HERE.
