# Engagement Brief — Astralis Cloud Services

**From:** Marcus Bauer, Lead Red Teamer, Vanguard Security
**To:** Junior Reconnaissance Specialist
**Re:** Active reconnaissance phase — Astralis Cloud Services

Astralis Cloud Services is a Dublin-headquartered EU-sovereign cloud provider
(~450 employees; datacenters in Dublin, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Stockholm). They
have just won a contract with a German federal entity conditional on an
independent penetration test. Vanguard holds the mandate.

Your package is the foundation of the next six weeks of operational work. The
Rules of Engagement (attached) authorise **moderate-intensity active
reconnaissance** against the corporate domains and their resolved
infrastructure. No SYN flood, no disruptive probing, no exploitation.

Twelve technical tasks, twelve one-line scripts, twelve flags. The Instructions
tell you *what* to find — not *how*. You choose the DNS record type, the `nmap`
flags, the parser, the API call. The flag proves the methodology.

Read Tomas Lindqvist's note in the project Task 0 before you start: the asset
that matters most is rarely on the front page.
