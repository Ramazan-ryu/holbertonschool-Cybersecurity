## 3. Evidence and Posture

The evidence pack shows both effective controls and material weaknesses. **Current-state conclusions below are based only on the supplied evidence pack [F01][F02][F03][F04][F05][F06].**

**[F01] Secrets management — Green.** Vireo has a central vault, MFA enforced for all access, and signed quarterly access reviews [F01]. The latest access review was completed five weeks ago [F01]. This provides current evidence that the control is operating and should be certified as a strength [F01].

**[F04] Ledger resilience — Green.** Vireo performs daily encrypted backups and monthly restore tests using a signed runbook [F04]. The latest restore test was completed three weeks ago and was successful [F04]. This provides current evidence of an operating recovery control and should be certified as a strength [F04].

**[F05] Security testing — Red.** Vireo's security policy requires the payment platform to undergo penetration testing at least annually, but the last recorded test was 26 months ago [F05]. The current evidence therefore shows that the documented requirement is not being met on schedule [F05]. This is the paper-compliance finding and should be reported as a material gap [F05].

**[F02] Incident readiness — Amber/Red.** Vireo currently has a 24/7 on-call rota and a tested escalation phone tree [F02]. However, Vireo currently has no incident classification procedure, no reporting templates, and no demonstrated staff awareness of the four-hour regulatory notification requirement [F02]. The evidence therefore supports partial operational readiness but does not support a conclusion that Vireo currently has a complete regulatory incident-reporting capability [F02].

**[F03] Certeva third-party risk — Red.** Certeva currently has a SOC 2 Type I report dated March 2024, with no more recent assurance evidence in the supplied vendor file [F03]. The current services agreement contains no breach-notification clause, and Certeva is absent from Vireo's vendor inventory or register [F03]. The evidence therefore shows a material current third-party oversight gap [F03].

**[F06] Risk governance — Red.** Vireo currently has no formal risk register and no risk appetite statement [F06]. Board minutes show no risk item on the agenda for four consecutive quarters, while risk decisions are currently made verbally by the CTO [F06]. The evidence therefore does not support a conclusion that Vireo currently has formal, documented risk governance [F06].

The current evidence supports **two certified strengths, [F01] and [F04], alongside material gaps in [F02], [F03], [F05] and [F06]**. These findings should be mapped separately to NIST CSF 2.0 and the applicable DORA areas; evidence of one control should not be treated as evidence that the broader regulatory framework is satisfied [F01][F02][F03][F04][F05][F06].
