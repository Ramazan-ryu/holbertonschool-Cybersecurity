# Physical & Human Security Plan - SecureHealth

## Goal
Secure the physical and human perimeter based on Field Notes findings.

---

## Immediate Actions (0 Cost)

- Close server room door
- Erase whiteboard passwords
- Implement screen lock policy
- Fix visitor check-in
- Collect spare keycards
- No unauthorized persons in server room

---

## Short-Term Actions (Low Cost)

- Fix AC unit in server room
- Keycard tracking system
- Privacy screen on glass
- Auto screen lock config
- Clean desk policy
- Visitor management

---

## Long-Term Strategic Improvements

- Move to proper data center/cloud
- Proper access control (badge+biometric)
- CCTV in sensitive areas
- Dedicated office vs co-working
- MDM for laptops
- Professional security audit

---

## TikTok / Social Media Incident Handling

- Request content deletion
- No photography policy
- Visitor escort training
- Signage requirements
- Delivery escort procedure
- Consider NDA for visitors

---

## Security Awareness Training

- Security awareness training
- Clean desk policy training
- Visitor handling procedures
- Social engineering awareness
- Reporting suspicious activity

---

## Notes

- Prioritize zero-cost actions first to address immediate vulnerabilities.

- Short-term low-cost actions strengthen the perimeter and reduce risks.

- Long-term strategic improvements address root causes and systemic security weaknesses.

- Human factors, verification culture, and insider threats are key considerations.

- Ensure all staff follow verification procedures for visitors and deliveries.

- Regularly review keycard assignments and remove unused or duplicate cards.

- Maintain logs for all visitor check-ins and server room access.

- Staff must lock screens when leaving workstations, even briefly.

- All sensitive information must be removed from whiteboards and desks.

- Periodic drills should simulate social engineering attempts.

- Encourage staff to report suspicious activity immediately.

- Perform audits of clean desk policy adherence.

- Verify AC and cooling systems function in server room regularly.

- Conduct periodic inspections of privacy screens and glass partitions.

- Confirm all CCTV and monitoring systems are operational (for long-term).

- Evaluate MDM solutions for mobile devices and laptops.

- Enforce proper access control policies with badges and optional biometrics.

- Periodically review professional security audit findings and recommendations.

- Ensure signage for photography restrictions is visible in all sensitive areas.

- Delivery personnel must be escorted at all times inside sensitive areas.

- NDA agreements for visitors should be considered for sensitive zones.

- Regular security awareness training must cover clean desk, visitor handling, and social engineering risks.

- Document all incidents related to physical security or social media exposure.

- Review and update the physical security plan annually.

- Align physical and human security measures with IT and network security policies.

- Integrate training, monitoring, and incident response to address human factors.

- Use role-based access control to limit unnecessary permissions.

- Ensure departing staff accounts are removed and access revoked.

- Limit server room access to essential personnel only.

- Maintain backups of security logs and access records.

- Verify that AC, power, and cooling systems support all critical infrastructure.

- Use checklists for daily security routines and inspections.

- Communicate policies clearly to all employees and contractors.

- Combine procedural, technical, and training measures for comprehensive security.

- Evaluate effectiveness of each measure periodically.

- Implement continuous improvement based on audit results and incidents.

- Encourage a culture of verification, accountability, and awareness.

- All staff must follow the visitor escort policy consistently.

- Staff must be trained on no photography and social media policies.

- Periodic testing of security procedures should include delivery scenarios.

- Integrate physical security with IT monitoring for holistic protection.

- Ensure high-risk areas are clearly marked and access is restricted.

- Maintain an incident log for any breaches or near-misses.

- Review and update the plan when new threats are identified.

- Staff accountability is critical for sustaining the security posture.

- Conduct annual refresher training on all physical security policies.

- Align training and procedures with compliance and regulatory requirements.

- Encourage proactive reporting and open communication regarding security issues.
