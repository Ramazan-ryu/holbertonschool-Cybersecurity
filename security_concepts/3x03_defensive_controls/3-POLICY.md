**Section 4.2: Prohibited Activities**

Users shall not engage in the following activities while utilizing company-owned systems, networks, or resources:

- **Cryptocurrency Mining:** Cryptocurrency mining, crypto-mining, and any mining of digital currency using company systems or network resources is strictly prohibited.

- **Unauthorized Software Installation:** Installing, downloading, or executing any software that has not been explicitly approved by the IT Department is strictly prohibited.

**Consequences of Violation:**  
Violations of these policies will result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination of employment, revocation of access privileges, and potential legal action.
