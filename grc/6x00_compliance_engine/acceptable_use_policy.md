# Acceptable Use Policy

Policy number: MD-AUP-001
Policy title: Acceptable Use Policy
Effective date: 2026-06-01
Review date: 2027-06-01
Owner: IT Security
Approved by: CISO
Classification: Internal Use
Applies to: All MedDefense employees, contractors, vendors with remote access, temporary clinical staff, interns, consultants, and any individual granted access to MedDefense information systems, networks, applications, devices, or data.

---

## Purpose

This policy establishes mandatory requirements for the acceptable use of MedDefense information systems, electronic protected health information (ePHI), and organizational technology resources.

This policy exists to protect patient information, support secure clinical operations, satisfy HIPAA Security Rule requirements, and reduce organizational risk.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

* employees
* contractors
* vendors with remote access
* temporary clinical staff
* agency personnel
* interns and trainees
* any authorized user of MedDefense systems

Compliance with this policy is a condition of employment, engagement, and continued access to MedDefense resources.

---

## Acceptable use

MedDefense systems, accounts, devices, applications, and network resources are provided solely for authorized MedDefense business activities and approved clinical care delivery.

Users may access only the information, systems, and resources required to perform assigned job responsibilities. All activity performed using MedDefense resources must comply with organizational policies, security requirements, regulatory obligations, and applicable law.

Each user is accountable for all actions performed using assigned credentials.

---

## Prohibited activities

The following activities are prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

### Patient information misuse

* Accessing patient records outside the active care relationship for that patient.
* Accessing records without a legitimate clinical, operational, or business need.
* Viewing records belonging to family members, friends, coworkers, or public figures without authorized justification.

### Credential misuse

* Sharing credentials or permitting another person to use your account.
* Using another individual's credentials.
* Circumventing authentication controls.

### Unauthorized software

* Installing unauthorized software on any MedDefense endpoint.
* Downloading or executing unapproved applications.
* Disabling security controls or endpoint protection software.

### Unauthorized devices

* Connecting personal devices to the clinical network without IT Security authorization.
* Connecting unauthorized storage devices, wireless equipment, or networking hardware.

### Unauthorized data transmission

* Transmitting MedDefense data using personal email.
* Transmitting MedDefense data using consumer cloud storage.
* Transmitting MedDefense data using messaging applications.
* Storing MedDefense information on unapproved devices or services.

### Unauthorized access attempts

* Attempting to access systems, data, or network segments beyond your assigned role.
* Attempting to elevate privileges or bypass access controls.
* Conducting unauthorized scanning, testing, or monitoring activities.

### AI and third-party services

* Entering or processing patient data into AI tools, external web services, or third-party platforms without an approved Business Associate Agreement.
* Uploading ePHI, confidential information, or regulated data to unapproved AI systems.
* Using external platforms that have not completed required security and compliance review.

### Malicious or disruptive activity

* Introducing malware or malicious code.
* Deliberately disrupting systems, applications, networks, or clinical operations.
* Using MedDefense resources for illegal, fraudulent, harassing, or abusive activities.

---

## PHI handling obligations

Access, use, disclose, and share patient information only when required to perform assigned duties. If information is not required to complete a task, it must not be accessed, viewed, disclosed, or retained.

Users must follow the HIPAA minimum necessary principle by limiting access, use, and disclosure of patient information to the minimum amount needed to perform authorized work.

---

## Remote access

Remote access is permitted only through MedDefense-approved methods and security controls.

Users accessing MedDefense systems remotely must:

* Use approved authentication mechanisms.
* Maintain required endpoint security controls.
* Protect systems from unauthorized viewing or use.

Accessing MedDefense systems from public or shared Wi-Fi networks is prohibited unless an approved VPN connection is active before any MedDefense resource is accessed.

---

## Reporting obligations

Users must immediately report:

* Suspicious email or phishing attempts.
* Anomalous system behavior.
* Lost or stolen devices.
* Accidental PHI disclosure.
* Suspected malware infections.
* Credential compromise.
* Unauthorized access events.

Reports must be made through:

1. An IT Security ticket.
2. A phone call to IT Security immediately upon discovery.

Users must not delay reporting while attempting to investigate or resolve an issue independently.

---

## Consequences of violation

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action under MedDefense Human Resources procedures up to and including termination of employment, contract termination, revocation of access privileges, and legal action where appropriate.

Violations involving patient information may constitute HIPAA violations and may result in personal civil, criminal, professional, or regulatory liability.

---

## Acknowledgment and review

This policy shall be reviewed annually by IT Security and approved by the CISO.

All workforce members must acknowledge this policy annually.

New employees, contractors, temporary clinical staff, and vendors requiring access must sign this policy during onboarding before system access is granted.
