# CISO Compliance Brief

## Current Compliance Posture

MedDefense is currently At Risk for the upcoming OCR desk audit. The organization has made meaningful progress by producing core governance documents and technical evidence, but there are still critical gaps that prevent us from demonstrating full compliance. In particular, we cannot yet prove consistent vendor governance, complete workforce security assurance, and fully closed policy controls required under HIPAA Security Rule expectations.

---

## Top Three Findings Before the Audit

We cannot demonstrate that all workforce members are bound by fully enforceable written security policies because key governance policies (Acceptable Use, Data Classification, Vendor Management) were only recently completed and are still in the final approval and distribution stage.

We cannot demonstrate full workforce security compliance because security awareness training completion is only 73%, below the required internal threshold, and there is no completed phishing simulation program or role-based validation evidence.

We cannot demonstrate compliant third-party risk governance for CloudVault Medical because the vendor has not yet executed a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), and critical security validation gaps remain (including outdated penetration testing and undisclosed disaster recovery location).

---

## What We Have Completed

- Drafted and structured enforceable governance policies: Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy
- Produced formal risk documentation including risk register updates and a risk treatment plan with assigned owners and target dates
- Completed a CloudVault Medical vendor risk assessment identifying specific compliance gaps and required contractual conditions
- Compiled audit-ready evidence mapping across HIPAA Security Rule requirements (audit evidence package)
